Autonomous Quadruped Robot & Locomotion Sim

12-DOF legged robotics platform built around an inverse kinematics solver and dynamic stability modeling. Simulated leg trajectories, center-of-mass stabilization, and ground reaction forces in real time. Works flawlessly in simulation, where gravity is a tidy constant and friction never betrays you.

Double Wishbone Suspension Geometry

Proof-of-Concept double wishbone assembly engineered in SolidWorks to control dynamic camber and stabilize a 500 mL sloshing payload over a 33 m track run. Peak mechanical over-engineering for a straight-line sprint, but the kinematic camber curves are undeniable.

SolidWorksKinematicsVehicle DynamicsChem-E-Car

WheelSafe Storage — Accessible Mobility Aid

Engineered an ergonomic, weatherproof wheelchair storage module for a client with Spina Bifida. Achieved a 3.25:1 load-to-weight ratio with a 0.4 kg carbon-fiber-reinforced structure. Top 3 out of 200+ teams at the McMaster Design Showcase.

Vision-Guided 2-DOF Robotic Arm

Built in 12 hours, which is roughly how long SolidWorks takes to open. Runs a real-time OpenCV hand-tracking pipeline that streams coordinate deltas over UDP to an ESP32, which turns them into tuned stepper pulses without jitter.

OpenCVESP32C++ / UDP

Real-Time Compensation Visualizer

Spoofer — iOS GPS Simulator

Windows desktop tool that moves an iPhone's GPS location for testing location-aware apps. Type an address, pick a bookmark, or send the phone walking a real street route at 5 km/h. It drives Apple's developer location service through pymobiledevice3 over USB or Wi-Fi, and picks the route back up if the Wi-Fi drops halfway. Also taught me that clicking inside a PowerShell window silently pauses whatever is running in it.
