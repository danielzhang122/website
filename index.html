Projects

Quadruped Robot & Locomotion Sim

A 12-servo, 3D-printable quadruped designed in Fusion 360, exported to URDF and simulated in MuJoCo. A trot gait controller makes it stand, walk and turn in place, and drives the real servos through a PCA9685 board on a Raspberry Pi.

Python, MuJoCo, Fusion 360, Raspberry Pi Repo

Double Wishbone Suspension

A proof-of-concept double wishbone assembly designed in SolidWorks for McMaster Chem-E-Car. It controls dynamic camber to keep a 500 mL sloshing payload stable over a 33 m run.

SolidWorks, kinematics, vehicle dynamics

WheelSafe Storage

An ergonomic, weatherproof wheelchair storage module designed for a client with spina bifida. The 0.4 kg carbon-fiber-reinforced structure reached a 3.25:1 load-to-weight ratio. Placed top 3 of 200+ teams at the McMaster Design Showcase.

Autodesk Inventor, client prototyping, carbon fiber

Vision-Guided 2-DOF Robotic Arm

Built in 12 hours. A real-time OpenCV hand-tracking pipeline streams coordinate deltas over UDP to an ESP32, which turns them into smooth stepper motor pulses.

OpenCV, ESP32, C++, UDP

Globe Ghost — iOS GPS Simulator

Sets an iPhone's actual GPS location, not just its IP address like a VPN does, for testing location-aware apps. Includes a live map, scripted routes and a geofence tester.

Python, pymobiledevice3, OpenStreetMap Repo

Real-Time Compensation Visualizer

A live Python dashboard that shows earnings adding up in real time, down to the microsecond. Built to put an exact dollar figure on a 45-minute meeting.

Python, GUI Repo
